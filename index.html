Dr. José Rizal

Explore the extraordinary life, timeless writings, and enduring legacy of the national hero of the Philippines.

Begin the Journey

The Quintessential Filipino

Dr. Jose Protacio Rizal Mercado y Alonso Realonda stands as a beacon of intellectual brilliance, unwavering patriotism, and profound sacrifice. His contributions transcended mere advocacy; he ignited the flames of Philippine nationalism through the power of the pen.

Fact I

A Polymath's Mind

Rizal was fluent in over 22 languages including Latin, Greek, German, French, English, Spanish, Japanese, and Hebrew.

Fact II

Ophthalmic Surgeon

He specialized in ophthalmology in Europe so he could personally operate on and restore his mother's failing eyesight.

Fact III

The Pen over the Sword

Unlike violent revolutionaries, Rizal firmly believed that true freedom and reform must be achieved through education and moral enlightenment.

Early Life & Family

Calamba Roots

Born on June 19, 1861, in Calamba, Laguna. Raised in a wealthy, educated agricultural family that instilled in him early values of devotion, learning, and justice.

Education & Mastery

Academic Brilliance

Studied at Ateneo Municipal de Manila and UST before traveling to Spain, France, and Germany to master ophthalmology, philosophy, and arts.

Global Travels

Observer of Nations

Journeyed through Asia, Europe, and America, gaining deep perspectives on democracy, human rights, and political reform that inspired his writings.

Propaganda Era

La Solidaridad

Co-founded the Propaganda Movement in Spain, writing incisive essays and articles calling for equal rights for Filipinos under Spanish rule.

Literary Works: The Pen as a Sword

Noli Me Tángere

Touch Me Not (1887)

A scathing exposure of Spanish clerical hypocrisy and colonial abuses. It awakened Filipino national consciousness and laid the moral groundwork for revolution.

El Filibusterismo

The Reign of Greed (1891)

The darker, politically charged sequel exploring the tragic consequences of failed reforms, anger, and armed rebellion through the sinister character of Simoun.

Timeline of Historic Events

June 19, 1861

Birth in Calamba

José Rizal is born in Calamba, Laguna, the seventh child of Francisco Mercado and Teodora Alonso.

March 1887

Publication of Noli

Rizal finishes and publishes Noli Me Tángere in Berlin, Germany with financial help from Maximo Viola.

July 3, 1892

La Liga Filipina

Founds La Liga Filipina in Tondo, Manila, advocating for peaceful civic unity and social reform.

December 30, 1896

Martyrdom at Bagumbayan

Rizal is executed by firing squad at Bagumbayan (now Rizal Park), solidifying his role as the national hero.
