## 📜 La Luz Rizal

An interactive, vintage-themed educational website dedicated to exploring the life, literary masterpieces, and enduring legacy of Dr. José Rizal, the national hero of the Philippines.

**Live Demo:** [la-luz-rizal.vercel.app](https://la-luz-rizal.vercel.app)


## 📌 Project Overview

This project was originally created last year in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the **Life and Works of Rizal** course. It has since been updated and enhanced with modern UI/UX design patterns, interactive web features, and refined aesthetic polish to serve as an engaging visual resource for students and historical enthusiasts alike.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **🏛️ Vintage Dark Colonial Aesthetics:** Customized color schemes featuring deep charcoal tones, rich gold accents, and colonial brown highlights suited to historical themes.
* **🃏 Interactive 3D Biography Flip Cards:** Discover key chapters of Rizal's life through smooth 3D flipping cards detailing his early life, education, travels, and literary career.
* **📜 Interactive Timeline:** Scroll-triggered, sequential reveal effect detailing major life events and milestones.
* **📚 Literary Works Spotlight:** Visual cards showcasing Rizal’s major novels (*Noli Me Tángere* and *El Filibusterismo*) with layered background images.
* **💡 Fast-Fact Carousel:** Dynamic interactive slider revealing historical facts about Rizal.
* **🧠 Interactive Rizal Quiz:** An engine testing knowledge of Rizal’s life, works, and historic impact with instant feedback and score tracking.


---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **HTML5:** Semantic site structure and layout.
* **CSS3:** Custom styles, CSS variables, Flexbox, Grid layout, dynamic 3D card flips, and custom animations.
* **JavaScript (Vanilla JS):** Modern ES6 logic for section Intersection Observers, custom quiz state, carousel interactions, and navigation highlighting.
* **AOS (Animate On Scroll) Library:** Used for scroll-triggered element animations.
* **Vercel:** Deployment platform.

## 🚀 Recent Improvements & Refinements

* **Scrolling Reveal Animation:** Replaced standard animations with repeating Intersection Observers for smooth section reveals when scrolling both down and back up.
* **Color Palette Overhaul:** Transitioned high-contrast red design elements into colonial dark brown shades (`#2B1B17` / `#3A231C`) to align with the historic theme.
* **Layout & Depth Polish:** Implemented locked aspect ratios and linear gradient overlays for all cards to guarantee text readability over background images.
